A. removal strategy (interior condition only; placebo and context stay random)

random: week-7 interior rows, not recomputed; token sha256("6908|interior|<frame_id>|<pred_idx>|<fraction>")
fps: keep n - num_removed interior points by farthest-point sampling in x, y, z; delete the rest
fps start: rng(seed).integers(n) over the sorted interior; seed from sha256("6908|fps|interior|<frame_id>|<pred_idx>|<fraction>")
fps ties: lower sorted interior index; a chosen point is never chosen twice
voxel grid: POINT_CLOUD_RANGE [0.0, -39.68, -3.0, 69.12, 39.68, 1.0], VOXEL_SIZE [0.16, 0.16, 4.0] (read from the config)

voxel order: rng(seed).permutation of the sorted occupied pillars; seed from sha256("6908|voxel|interior|<frame_id>|<pred_idx>|<fraction>")
voxel walk: delete whole pillars while they fit; the first pillar that would overshoot gives up a random subset of exactly the remainder (same rng); stop
